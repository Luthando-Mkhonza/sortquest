📖 How It Works

🎼 The Orchestra

Each musician card shows their instrument and a pitch number (10–99). Lower numbers = lower pitch. Your goal: arrange all musicians from lowest to highest pitch so the orchestra can play in harmony.

🎻 Bubble Sort — Step by Step

  • Click Compare to highlight two adjacent musicians
  • If the left pitch is greater, click Swap to exchange them
  • Continue until the end of the unsorted section (one pass)
  • Click Next Pass to start a new round from the beginning
  • Sorted musicians glow green — once all are green, you win!
  • Use Auto-Run to watch the algorithm execute automatically

🎷 Quick Sort — Step by Step

  • Click Select Pivot to enter pivot-selection mode
  • Click any highlighted musician to set them as the pivot (glows purple)
  • Click Partition to preview: teal = smaller than pivot, red = larger
  • Click Apply to physically rearrange — the pivot lands in its final sorted spot
  • Repeat for the remaining unsorted sub-sections until all are sorted!
